CORE GAMEPLAY

WAGER → SPIN → CASCADE → SETTLE
  1. Wager – Choose stake between 10 and 1,000 credits.
  2. Spin – 20 positions evaluated left to right.
  3. Cascade – Winning symbols explode, new symbols tumble down.
  4. Settle – Bank credits or enter 50/50 Gamble ladder.

CASCADING GAMEPLAY

Cascading Drops

  • Winning symbols explode.
  • Empty positions are created.
  • Remaining symbols tumble downward.
  • New symbols enter the grid.
  • New winning combinations trigger additional cascades.

Avalanche Multiplier

Every consecutive cascade increases the global spin win multiplier:

x1 → x2 → x3 → x4 → x5

Maximum multiplier x5 during avalanche sequence.
